How do I print an article or crossword puzzle?

From the app, follow these steps:

Apple users, go to the Crossword Puzzle, tap the Share button then Print. OR, take a screenshot/snippet, then print.

From your browser, follow these steps:

First, Select the 'Print' icon in the lower right. You can enlarge your page using the magnifier tool in the bottom tool bar.

Next, select 'Clip' and then choose and highlight the section of the page you want to clip. Set the print layout to Landscape for best results printing Crosswords.

How can I search for an article?

The online newspaper has an archive of past editions. To find a story, use the ‘Search’ feature located in the upper right toolbar of the online newspaper.

Tap Search.

Find the 'Search' at the top of the screen. If you're on a mobile device, you'll see three bars in the upper right. Click to open.

The Search tool will open a dialog box.

Enter your search term. You can choose 'Most Recent' or 'Most Relevant'. Then, select the time period for the search: All time, Past day, Past week, Past month, or Past year.

Articles with your search term will show along with their publication date and time. Click on any story to view it.

How do I save an article or page?

Create a library of your favorites. Click on this button to save a page or article for reference later.

Saving Pages.

In Page View, click on the ‘Save’ button to save a page for reference later. The button will be shaded black once the Save is complete. You can access Saved pages or articles in the Saved tab in the upper right toolbar.

Saving Stories.

With the story open, click on the ‘Save’ button to save the article for later. The button will be shaded black once the Save is complete. You can access Saved pages or articles in the Saved tab in the upper right toolbar.

To remove pages or articles, click on the ‘Save’ button again to remove it from your Saved Content.

How do I access exclusive content?

The online newspaper features exclusive content that is not available in the printed edition. Additional content is available in the ‘Supplements’ and ‘Specials’ tabs located on the homepage of the online newspaper.

Supplements include The New York Times Book Review and the Weekly TV Best Bets are updated each Sunday and archived for reference. A User Guide is also provided as a resource.

Specials feature online-exclusive sections on Food, Travel, Home & Garden, and Sports. These sections are archived for reader enjoyment. Watch for exciting seasonal offerings!
